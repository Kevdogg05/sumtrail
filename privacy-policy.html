Sum Trail privacy policy

Effective [October 14, 2026]

Sum Trail ("the app") is made by Trailhead Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own.

Your game progress (levels reached, stars, hints, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, app set ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ad interactions, and diagnostic data, in order to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states), the app asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tools. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices (shown where applicable).

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).

Ratings

The app may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating card. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play under Google's policies; we only see what Google Play publicly shows.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising and Google Play services described above, we do not share information with third parties.

Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
